About

Each UKB-PPP Olink cis-pQTL credible set is graded by transcript-level support (colocalisation with a cognate eQTL, PP.H4 ≥ 0.8) and by whether it contains a protein-altering variant (PAV) of the cognate gene. Replication on SomaScan cohorts, mass-spectrometry labels and cross-ancestry replication are listed alongside. Tiers follow fixed rules from a pre-specified analysis plan (Zenodo).

Evidence tiers

  • Tier 1eQTL-anchored, no PAV in the 95% credible set. n = 2,539
  • Tier 2eQTL-anchored with a PAV, or anchored to an sQTL/tuQTL only. n = 682
  • Tier 3No anchor and no PAV: unresolved. n = 10,046
  • Tier 4No anchor, with PAV: affinity data alone cannot separate an abundance effect from an epitope effect. This is not a false-positive call. n = 1,205
  • FlagConcordant + unanchored + PAV: replicated on SomaScan, yet unanchored and PAV-containing; the class most in need of confirmation. n = 339

Notes

  • Tiers are not mass-spectrometry (MS) ground truth. MS labels exist for few, mostly strong signals.
  • Both MS label sets (MSPA and Seer full summary statistics) come from one Seer Proteograph study and are not independent.
  • deCODE, ARIC and Fenland all use SomaScan v4; agreement among them is cross-cohort, not orthogonal, evidence.
  • Only derived per-credible-set statistics are provided. rsIDs are taken from the Open Targets Platform 26.09 variant index.
