About

This resource accompanies the paper Protein-altering variants inflate cross-platform replication of plasma protein QTLs (in submission). Each UKB-PPP Olink cis-pQTL credible set carries two annotations: whether its 95% credible set contains a protein-altering variant (PAV) of the cognate gene, and whether it has transcript support, that is, colocalisation with a cognate eQTL (PP.H4 ≥ 0.8). The four tiers are combinations of these two annotations, assigned by fixed rules from a pre-specified analysis plan (Zenodo). They are not a validated reliability ranking: transcript support did not predict mass-spectrometry confirmation. Replication on SomaScan cohorts, mass-spectrometry (MS) labels from four layers and cross-ancestry replication are listed alongside.

Evidence tiers

  • Tier 1Transcript support, no PAV in the 95% credible set. n = 2,539
  • Tier 2Transcript support with a PAV, or support from a transcript-isoform QTL (sQTL/tuQTL) only. n = 682
  • Tier 3Neither transcript support nor a PAV. n = 10,046
  • Tier 4A PAV without transcript support: affinity data alone cannot separate an abundance effect from an epitope effect. This is not a false-positive call. n = 1,205
  • FlagConcordant + unanchored + PAV: replicated on SomaScan, yet without transcript support and with a PAV. n = 339

Notes

  • MS labels come from four layers. MSPA and the Seer full summary statistics come from one Seer Proteograph study and are not independent. Generation Scotland and HOLBAEK are separate MS studies. HOLBAEK released statistics only for proteins with a pQTL; other proteins are “not evaluable”.
  • Credible sets on chromosome X (500) have no MS data, because no MS layer releases chrX summary statistics.
  • MS labels exist for a minority of mostly strong signals, and MS measures peptides, so MS support for a PAV-containing signal does not by itself show an abundance effect.
  • Platform concordance is higher for PAV-containing signals, so for these signals it is not orthogonal validation. Overall, concordance was positively associated with MS support in Generation Scotland and HOLBAEK (combined OR 2.92) but only weakly, and not significantly, in the two Seer layers.
  • deCODE, ARIC and Fenland all use SomaScan v4; agreement among them is cross-cohort, not orthogonal, evidence.
  • Only derived per-credible-set statistics are provided. rsIDs are taken from the Open Targets Platform 26.09 variant index.
