Protein-altering variants inflate agreement between affinity proteomics platforms for plasma cis-pQTLs: a pre-registered evaluation against transcript, mass-spectrometry and cross-ancestry evidence

Jiayao Ni, Wanqiang Zhang, Tao Zhang* · The First Affiliated Hospital of Nanjing Medical University · in submission

How to read the tiers

Tiers are fixed rules from the frozen pre-registration; no model is trained. “Anchored” = colocalisation with a cognate GTEx v10 or eQTL Catalogue eQTL (PP.H4 ≥ 0.8). “PAV” = the 95% credible set contains a protein-altering variant of the cognate gene (VEP 116, any transcript).

T1Transcript-anchored, no PAVeQTL-anchored and no cognate PAV in the credible set. Transcript-level support suggests an abundance mechanism.
T2Anchored with PAV, or isoform-onlyeQTL-anchored but the set also contains a PAV, or anchored only to an sQTL/tuQTL.
T3Unresolved: no anchor, no PAVNo transcript anchor and no PAV; the mechanism cannot be classified from these features.
T4Caution: no anchor, with PAVNo anchor and the set contains a PAV, regardless of platform concordance. Affinity data alone cannot separate a true abundance effect from an epitope effect.

T4 is not a false-positive call. Functional missense variants (e.g. in PCSK9) also fall into T4; such signals need mass-spectrometry or other orthogonal evidence. The flag concordant + unanchored + PAV marks 339 credible sets that replicate on SomaScan but carry no anchor and a PAV — the key class to confirm.

Cautions

  • Not mass-spectrometry ground truth. A tier summarises transcript and coding-variant evidence; it is not an MS verdict. MS labels exist for few, mostly strong signals (MSPA primary label evaluable for 169 credible sets; Seer full-statistics primary label for 367).
  • The two MS layers are not independent. The MSPA labels (Suhre et al. 2025 supplementary tables) and the labels from the Seer full summary statistics (GWAS Catalog GCST90570713–GCST90572692) come from the same Seer Proteograph MS study (primary-label agreement 94.6%, κ = 0.89, n = 148). Agreement between them is not replication.
  • Platform concordance is not orthogonal validation. deCODE, ARIC and Fenland are all SomaScan v4; replication across them is cross-cohort, not cross-platform. Binary cross-platform replication is higher for PAV-containing signals even after power matching, while effect agreement is lower.
  • Derived statistics only. The portal and resource contain per-credible-set derived statistics and annotations. No third-party summary-statistic files are redistributed. Colocalisation uses per-variant log Bayes factors within Open Targets 99% credible sets (approximate); MAF is from gnomAD non-Finnish Europeans; 204 credible sets whose Open Targets β is inconsistent with P and MAF are flagged (post hoc).
  • Not for clinical decision-making. Model probabilities in the full resource (p_ms_support_model) have poor external validity and are deliberately not shown here.
